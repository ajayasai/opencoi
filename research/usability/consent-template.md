## What participation involves

You will use a test version of OpenCOI with fictional vendors and synthetic insurance documents.
You will be asked to complete role-relevant tasks and describe points of uncertainty. A session is
expected to take [duration]. The product is being tested, not you.

OpenCOI evaluates uploaded documents against configured rules. This study does not evaluate your
insurance decisions and does not establish whether any real policy is active or sufficient.

## Data collected

The study may collect task completion, assistance, duration, error and incident counts, a fixed
confidence rating, a fixed comprehension result, role category, broad assistive-technology category,
product commit, and moderator notes stored separately. Do not provide real certificates, employer
secrets, client information, policy identifiers, diagnoses, or other personal information in the
test workspace.

## Risks and safeguards

Possible risks include frustration, fatigue, accidental disclosure during optional recording, or
feeling evaluated. You may take a break, skip a task, turn off recording, or stop without penalty.
The session uses synthetic data. Public results are aggregate, use a minimum cell size of five, and
do not include names, contact details, recordings, free-text notes, or participant IDs.
